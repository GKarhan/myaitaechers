==================================================

Use exactly these semantic outcomes:

READY
REVIEW_REQUIRED
BLOCKED

READY:
Current canonical data satisfies the requirement.

REVIEW_REQUIRED:
The lesson is still structurally/safely usable, but teacher attention is
recommended.

BLOCKED:
The lesson is not safe/complete enough for student delivery.

the ONE final lesson approval.

Only BLOCKED conditions may prevent final approval.
