0 — FROZEN PRODUCT DECISION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The approved Mapping workflow is:

Teacher may optionally provide:
- Lesson Goal
- draft Lesson Outcomes

BUT teacher-entered Goal/Outcomes are INPUT/DRAFT,
not automatically the final canonical curriculum definition.

The system/AI MUST still inspect the source lesson.

If teacher Goal/Outcomes exist:

source lesson
+ teacher draft Goal/Outcomes

→ AI/source-aware analysis

→ proposed revised/completed Goal
→ proposed revised/completed Outcomes

The proposal may:
- preserve good teacher wording
- improve wording
- add missing outcomes
- merge duplicated outcomes
- split an outcome that is too broad
- flag coverage gaps

BUT AI MUST NOT approve curriculum outcomes.

If teacher Goal/Outcomes do NOT exist:

source lesson
→ AI proposes Lesson Goal + Lesson Outcomes.

IN BOTH CASES:

AI proposal
→ Teacher Review
→ teacher may edit/add/delete/reorder
→ Teacher explicitly CONFIRMS

ONLY AFTER CONFIRMATION:

→ detailed Topic/MicroNode Mapping may proceed.

This approval gate is mandatory for the NEW canonical workflow.

Do not silently overwrite teacher input.

Do not silently approve AI proposals.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════

source lesson.

Its job is to help detect:

- missing important curriculum content
- overly broad outcome

- duplicate outcome
- outcome not supported by source

not automatic authority.
