2. RESTORE THE CANONICAL PRODUCT CONTRACT
==================================================

There must be ONE canonical distinction:

A. HARD BLOCKER

Only conditions that make assignment technically or structurally unsafe/impossible may prevent assignment.

Examples may include, only if actually required by the existing architecture:

- lesson does not exist;
- lesson has no usable MicroNodes at all;
- required persisted structure is corrupt/inconsistent;
- assignment cannot create a valid student-facing assignment;
- other truly fatal invariant violations.

Do not invent new blockers.

B. REVIEW/WARNING

Quality/review issues must NOT automatically prevent assignment.

Examples include:

- readable source blocks without MicroNode ownership;
- source review required;
- MicroNode review required;
- Cognitive Path missing/review required;
- teaching content missing/review required;
- exercises not attached;
- draft/review statuses;
- other non-fatal quality warnings.

These must remain visible to the teacher but must not silently become hard blockers.
