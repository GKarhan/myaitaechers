7. DO NOT DETERMINE LEVEL FROM VERB ALONE
==================================================

Avoid simple verb lookup architecture.

Verbs are signals, not sufficient truth.

Example:

"որոշել"
can mean recall, application, or analysis depending on the object/task.

"կառուցել"
may indicate application or create depending on constraints/originality.

"բացատրել"
may indicate understand, but context matters.

"վերափոխել"
can imply application when the learner must execute a procedure.

Use semantic performance + required evidence.

If deterministic Armenian verb patterns exist today,
keep them as weak/medium signals, not authoritative final classification.

==================================================
8. PERFORMANCE-FIRST RESOLUTION
==================================================

Resolve demand by identifying the required learner performance.

Conceptual questions:

1. What INPUT is the learner given?
2. What OUTPUT/action must the learner produce?
3. Is recall sufficient?
4. Must the learner interpret/explain?
5. Must the learner execute a known procedure?
6. Must the learner decompose/compare/infer structure?
7. Must the learner judge using criteria?
8. Must the learner construct something genuinely novel within the curriculum target?

Map the required performance to Bloom conservatively.

Examples:

Recall definition:
REMEMBER

Explain relationship:
UNDERSTAND

Use known rule/procedure on a new routine instance:
APPLY

Interpret/decompose/compare relationships:
ANALYZE

Judge correctness/quality using explicit criteria:
EVALUATE

Produce novel solution/model/design where construction itself is target:
CREATE

Do not over-classify routine construction exercises as CREATE automatically.
