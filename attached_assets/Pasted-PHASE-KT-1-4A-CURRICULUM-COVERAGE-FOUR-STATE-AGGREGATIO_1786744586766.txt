PHASE KT-1.4A — CURRICULUM COVERAGE + FOUR-STATE AGGREGATION

IMPORTANT PRODUCT DECISION — LOCKED

We are changing the Knowledge Tree progress model.

DO NOT collapse learner progress into one generic "mastery %" by simply
averaging current masteryScore values.

The product now separates TWO fundamentally different dimensions:

1. CURRICULUM COVERAGE
   How much of the curriculum the learner has actually studied.

2. COGNITIVE DEPTH
   How deeply the learner can work with each atomic knowledge unit.

KT-1.4A implements ONLY dimension #1 plus the existing four-state
distribution.

Cognitive Depth will be implemented later after Phase 2 creates the
required per-MicroNode cognitive/Bloom structure.

7. COVERAGE FORMULA — LOCKED CONCEPT
==================================================

Coverage answers:

"How much of this curriculum scope has the learner studied?"

Formula:

coveragePercent =
round(
  studiedCount / totalUnits × 100
)

Example:

100 total MicroNodes
40 studied
60 not studied

Coverage:

40%

This DOES NOT mean the learner knows 40%.

It means:

40% of the curriculum has been studied.

Null / dash means there are no atomic curriculum units in this scope.
