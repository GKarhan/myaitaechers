==================================================
GOAL
==================================================

For every generated quiz question shown to the TEACHER during quiz review/editing, display:

1. The MicroNode / Knowledge Node that the question assesses.
2. The canonical Cognitive Level that the question assesses.

Example:

🧩 Գիտելիքի հանգույց՝ Անհայտ զանգվածի որոշումը նժարավոր կշեռքով
🧠 Ճանաչողական մակարդակ՝ Կիրառել

These labels MUST come from persisted authoritative provenance:

quiz_question.relatedNodeId / nodeId
→ lesson_nodes

quiz_question.cognitiveLevelId
→ lesson_node_cognitive_levels

Do NOT infer either label from question text.
Do NOT ask the AI/provider to generate these labels.
Do NOT infer Bloom from difficultyMode.
