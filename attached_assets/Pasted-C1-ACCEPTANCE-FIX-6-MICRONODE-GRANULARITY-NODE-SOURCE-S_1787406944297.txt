6. CANONICAL MICRONODE DEFINITION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A MicroNode is:

ONE pedagogically distinct,
independently teachable,
independently assessable,
source-grounded learning objective.

It is NOT:

- one paragraph
- one source block
- one heading
- one example
- one story fragment
- one exercise
- one representation
- one Outcome wording
- one arbitrary AI topic fragment

Several source blocks may support one MicroNode.

Several exercises may assess one MicroNode.

Several Outcomes may relate to one MicroNode.

But the MicroNode itself represents ONE coherent knowledge/skill target.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. STRONG DUPLICATE / NEAR-DUPLICATE TEST
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before persistence, compare proposed MicroNodes within the same lesson,
especially within the same Topic.

Two proposed MicroNodes should normally be MERGED when:

A. they teach the same underlying concept/rule/skill;

B. their learningObjectives differ mainly by wording;

C. one objective is a narrower restatement of the other;

D. they own different source blocks that explain/example the same
   knowledge;

E. they would naturally be taught together;

F. they would naturally be assessed by substantially the same evidence;

G. mastering one would substantially demonstrate mastery of the other;

H. one is merely:
   - context
   - example
   - representation
   - narrative
   - explanation
   of the other's objective.

Do not rely only on title string similarity.

Use:

- title
- learningObjective
- semantic intent
- owned source blocks
- confirmed Outcome relations
- target cognitive depth

to determine pedagogical equivalence.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9. DO NOT OVER-MERGE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Two MicroNodes must remain separate when they represent genuinely
independent learning objectives.

Examples conceptually:

KNOWING WHY addresses must uniquely identify buildings

is not necessarily the same objective as

APPLYING the odd/even side-of-street numbering rule.

Similarly:

UNDERSTANDING the odd/even street rule

may be distinct from

UNDERSTANDING the special rule for newly constructed buildings between
existing addresses.

Merge semantic duplicates.

Do NOT collapse the entire lesson into one broad node.
