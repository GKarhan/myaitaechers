════════════════════════════════════════════
0. PRODUCT GOAL
════════════════════════════════════════════

The current authoring workflow has become too approval-heavy.

Today, ordinary teacher edits can cause:
- lesson approval invalidation;
- needs_review;
- repeated Final Approval;
- repeated Phase 2 generation;
- unnecessary teacher clicks;
- unnecessary AI cost.

We want a much simpler model.

INITIAL LESSON PREPARATION happens once:

Mapping
    ↓
Teacher Review
    ↓
Phase 2 enrichment
    ↓
Final Approval
    ↓
Lesson is ready

AFTER THAT:

Teacher is the final authority.

Normal teacher edits should behave like normal editing:

EDIT
    ↓
SAVE
    ↓
DB updated
    ↓
UI immediately reflects saved data

NO additional approval click should normally be required.

Core principle:

    SAVE = AUTHORITATIVE TEACHER UPDATE

Do not make the teacher repeatedly approve their own edits.

2. NEW AUTHORITATIVE APPROVAL RULE
════════════════════════════════════════════

Final Approval is primarily an INITIAL AUTHORING GATE.

Required initial lifecycle:

Mapping
    ↓
Teacher Review
    ↓
Phase 2 enrichment
    ↓
Final Approval
    ↓
READY

Once the lesson has successfully passed Final Approval:

ordinary teacher authoring changes must NOT automatically send the entire
lesson back through the initial approval workflow.

Specifically, after initial approval, the following must NOT automatically:

- set the whole lesson to needs_review;
- remove approved/ready state;
- require whole-lesson Final Approval again;
- require whole-lesson Phase 2 regeneration;
- trigger AI generation;
- block the teacher with another approval workflow.

Teacher explicitly saved the edit.

That saved edit is authoritative.

════════════════════════════════════════════

- second click must NOT create another job.
