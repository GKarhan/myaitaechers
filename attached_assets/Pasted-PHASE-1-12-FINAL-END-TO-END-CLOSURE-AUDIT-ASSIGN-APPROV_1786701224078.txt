8. SAME QUIZ RE-RELEASE — CRITICAL
════════════════════════════════════════════

A completed Quiz X must NOT become permanently unavailable forever.

Required lifecycle:

Teacher releases Quiz X
    ↓
Student sees «Սկսել թեստը»
    ↓
Student completes Quiz X
    ↓
Quiz X disappears from lesson actionable tests
    ↓
Attempt/result remains in «Իմ թեստերը»
    ↓
Teacher explicitly assigns/releases SAME Quiz X again
    ↓
Quiz X becomes actionable under lesson again
    ↓
Student sees «Սկսել թեստը»
    ↓
Student may complete another attempt

CRITICAL:

Do NOT determine current completion using:

    "Has this student EVER completed Quiz X?"

That would break re-release.

Completion must correspond to the CURRENT release/assignment cycle according
to the authoritative existing DB model.

Historical attempts/results must remain preserved.

Do NOT duplicate the Quiz itself merely to re-release it.

If current schema genuinely cannot distinguish release cycles, STOP before
inventing a workaround and report:

1. exact schema limitation;
2. why existing assignment + attempt records are insufficient;
3. smallest safe model/schema change required.
