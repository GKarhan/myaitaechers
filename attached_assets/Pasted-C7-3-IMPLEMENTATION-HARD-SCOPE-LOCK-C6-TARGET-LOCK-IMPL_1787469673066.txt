0. NON-NEGOTIABLE TARGET AUTHORITY
==================================================

The authoritative instructional target is server state derived from C6:

- lessonId
- currentNodeId
- activeCognitiveLevelId

and the accepted C2 path data for that level.

C7 may choose HOW to teach inside that target.

C7 MUST NOT independently choose:

- another lesson
- another MicroNode
- another Cognitive Level
- a higher Bloom level
- a lower curriculum level
- another prerequisite
- the next node
- a different curriculum objective

Only an authorized C7.1 → C3 → C4 → C6 transition may change
currentNodeId or activeCognitiveLevelId.

==================================================
6. LESSON / TOPIC SCOPE LOCK
==================================================

The AI Teacher must not teach another lesson or unrelated topic.

If learner asks:

“անցնենք ուրիշ թեմայի”
“սովորեցրու հաջորդ դասը”
or asks an unrelated curriculum question,

the system must:

- preserve currentNodeId
- preserve activeCognitiveLevelId
- not generate evidence for another target
- not silently switch context
- respond with a short warm redirection to the current target
- set/use redirect_needed where the existing contract requires it.

Do not let the model decide whether scope may change.

==================================================
7. LEARNER QUESTIONS INSIDE CURRENT SCOPE
==================================================

Do NOT over-lock normal teaching.

The learner may ask clarification questions about the current target.

C7 should answer normally.

Scope lock must prevent curriculum drift,
not prevent useful clarification.
