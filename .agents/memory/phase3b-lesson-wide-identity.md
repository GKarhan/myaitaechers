description: Canonical lesson-wide MicroNode identity, conflict safety, and deterministic final Topic assignment.
---

Canonical knowledge identity is lesson-wide. Provisional Topics supply organizational context only; an explicit high-confidence semantic same-knowledge decision may combine eligible candidates across Topic boundaries.

**Why:** The same learner knowledge state can be introduced, explained, and practiced across multiple sections. Treating Topic boundaries as identity boundaries creates duplicate learner-tracked MicroNodes and loses the distinction between knowledge identity and cognitive depth.

**How to apply:** Use heuristics only to retrieve suspicion pairs and reuse the bounded semantic-review decision as merge authority. Build deterministic groups, union source/support/exercise evidence into one source-grounded representative, and assign its final Topic by first substantive source introduction, then core-evidence strength, then stable ordering. A distinct or unresolved edge that contradicts a same-knowledge group forces source-only review; never merge it transitively. Keep provider calls bounded and preserve all verified material.
