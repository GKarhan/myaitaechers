## Rule
When searching for the Armenian "and" connector in LO text, match U+0587 (ARMENIAN SMALL LIGATURE ECH YIWN), not the 3-char Latin "yev".

## Why
The DB stores Armenian LO text in proper Unicode (generated by the AI mapping pipeline). The word "and" in Eastern Armenian appears as U+0587, a single-character ligature. Searching for the Latin 3-char sequence "y"+"e"+"v" will always miss it.

## How to apply
- In `granularity-heuristics.ts` COMPOUND_CONNECTORS: use `"\u0587"` as the first entry — this is already done.
- In frontend `_P15_CONNECTORS` (teacher-dashboard.tsx): use `"\u0587"` — already fixed.
- In any test LOs written in transliteration (e.g. "ev"), replace with `"\u0587"` when testing the Armenian connector branch, or use English " and " for a language-independent test.
- Proof: node 1351 LO confirmed via DB codepoint dump — connector word `"yev" → U+0587`.
