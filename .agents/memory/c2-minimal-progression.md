---
name: C2 minimal progression
description: The durable C2-3 rules for minimal Cognitive Paths, prerequisite checkpoints, and safe persistence.
---

The progression policy preserves the accepted target as the sole final Cognitive Path ceiling. It permits non-contiguous paths and target-only paths when the available source evidence does not establish an independent lower prerequisite. A generated path may persist only when a deterministic progression decision is present and marked minimal with no review reasons.

**Why:** Mechanical Bloom ladders make paths longer without adding assessable learning value; the inverse risk is silently omitting a lower checkpoint that source evidence makes independently necessary. The decision must therefore fail closed on both redundant and missing prerequisites.

**How to apply:** Infer lower checkpoints only from verified theory/exercise evidence: independent conceptual content or explicit factual/procedural/analytic/evaluative dependency language. Never infer them from the target action, title, or objective alone. Require each inferred checkpoint to show the matching competency and a concrete source-term connection in its own objective or criterion. Check every level's action demand against that level's Bloom rank, while distinguishing Armenian action forms from noun/reference forms (for example, constructive `կազմել` versus structural `կազմը`). Keep the decision in the C2 job ledger and route review-required paths away from persistence; do not create exercise/task attachments as part of C2 path generation.
