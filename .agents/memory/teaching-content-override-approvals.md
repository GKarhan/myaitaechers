---
name: Teaching Content override approvals
description: Rules for approving an otherwise safe lesson when generated Teaching Content remains incomplete.
---

Missing Teaching Content is overrideable only through an explicit teacher confirmation after all other final-approval checks pass. An override records the factual absence; it must never fabricate, replace, or mark the missing content as generated.

**Why:** Provider candidates can be rejected for unsupported claims even when the mapped lesson itself is safe and source-grounded. Blocking that lesson forever would make an operational generation failure the final authority, while allowing a silent bypass would erase the teacher’s decision and delivery safeguards.

**How to apply:** Keep normal approvals sticky, but keep missing-content overrides non-sticky. Any material authoring edit—including delivery-active lessons—must return a non-sticky override approval to review before students can continue receiving it. Missing content is the only overrideable issue.
