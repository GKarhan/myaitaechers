---
name: Teaching Content override approvals
description: Rules for approving an otherwise safe lesson when generated Teaching Content remains incomplete.
---

Missing Teaching Content and other bounded pedagogical-completeness findings are overrideable only through an explicit teacher confirmation after all hard final-approval checks pass. An override records factual finding codes; it must never fabricate, replace, or mark missing content as generated.

**Why:** Provider candidates can be rejected for unsupported claims even when the mapped lesson itself is safe and source-grounded. Blocking that lesson forever would make an operational generation failure the final authority, while allowing a silent bypass would erase the teacher’s decision and delivery safeguards.

**How to apply:** Keep normal approvals sticky, but keep review-confirmed approvals non-sticky. Any material authoring edit—including delivery-active lessons—must return a non-sticky override approval to review before students can continue receiving it. Only safe pedagogical findings are confirmable; structural, source-integrity, identity, and delivery-safety failures are never overrideable.
